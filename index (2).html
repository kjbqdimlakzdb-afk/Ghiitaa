How it began

A date on the calendar that turned into everything else

Some days pass through you and are forgotten by evening. The 25th of January wasn't one of them. It's strange how a single ordinary day can quietly decide to become the one you measure every other day against.

There was no plan for it to matter this much. And yet here we are, still counting from it, still finding new reasons it was the beginning of something worth keeping.

Her work

The hands that hold everything together

She spends her days in a room most people never see — where the lights are too bright, the stakes are too high, and there's no room for a hand that trembles. She stands there anyway, calm, precise, present for strangers on the worst day of their lives.

I used to think that kind of steadiness would run out by the time she got home. It never has. Somehow, after holding a whole operating room together, she still has enough left over to hold this — us — together too.

"You spend your shifts keeping other people's hearts steady, and somehow mine has never felt safer than it does with you."

A note, for you

Ghita,

I don't know how to write this without it sounding smaller than it is, so I'll just say it plainly: since the 25th of January, everything has had a little more color in it.

I love the version of you that walks into a room full of pressure and makes it calmer just by being in it. I love that you carry that same calm home, even on the days it costs you the most. I love that you chose to spend your rest — the little of it you get — on me.

This page is a small thing next to what you do every day. But I wanted a record of it somewhere: that a certain nurse, on a certain day, started something in me I'm in no hurry to end.

Here's to every day after this one.

Yours, always
